## Findings

- **P1 · `summary_large_image` card with no image** — `app/[locale]/questions/[id]/page.tsx:71` sets `twitter: { card: 'summary_large_image' }` and neither the page nor the layout emits `og:image`/`twitter:image` (verified in rendered head: 6 og tags, no image). On the surface whose privacy note argues "the same page can be shared across the spectrum," share cards degrade to an imageless fallback. Fix: ship an OG image or declare `summary`. Owner call on which (decision).
- **P2 · Caption idiom stretched to paragraph length** — `moments.aiNote` (29 words EN, longer ES) renders through the `ai` chip as 6 lines of 12px uppercase 0.08em-tracked text at 390 (measured 99px). `bills.aiNote` on the vehicles grid has the same shape. Long uppercase tracked passages are measurably harder to read; the chip was designed for captions like "AI-DECODED". Trim the string or give long disclosures a sentence-case treatment. Taste/owner call.
- **P3 · "Why this Big Question exists" outline/visual mismatch** — `[id]/page.tsx:379`: an H2 set at `text-xs` uppercase renders smaller than the H3 day headings above it; the visual says "appendix label", the outline says "peer of The bills". Deliberate demotion, but AT-user and sighted-user hierarchies diverge. Taste call.
- **P3 · Timeline has no list semantics** — `MomentTimeline.tsx:160-226`: day rows are `<p>`/`<div>` siblings, so screen readers announce no count/position for the ledger (updates within a day do get an `<ol>`). Wrap the ledger in a list or add a landmark description.
- **P3 · Skip link 41.6px focus-revealed height** — site-wide layout component (out of this surface's strict scope), under the project's own 44px floor.
- **P3 · Mobile record page is ~8.7 screens with no in-page nav** — /questions/iran-war-powers at 390 is 7330px. Fine for a record; a section jump list would serve the register. Taste call.
- **P3 · "Where it stands — July 26" under "Data as of August 2"** — the 7-day-old revision date beside a today freshness claim can read as contradiction; it is honest (reanchor fires at 7d — the funding page reanchored 08-01) but the gap is at its widest exactly when a reader notices. Data cadence, not UI; no action urged.
